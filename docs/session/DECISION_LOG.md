## Recording Rules

- Record decisions only from an identified authority.
- Preserve the original decision wording and conditions.
- Do not infer approval from discussion or execution.
- Record supersession without deleting historical decisions.
- Link each decision to its authorization and evidence.
- A decision applies only within its stated scope.
